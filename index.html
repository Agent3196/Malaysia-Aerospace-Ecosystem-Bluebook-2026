⚠ Capability Gap — CFM56 Full Engine Shop

No CAAM-approved full CFM56-5B or CFM56-7B engine shop is currently listed in Malaysia. Major overhauls are routed to ST Engineering (Singapore), Lufthansa Technik (Germany), or Air France Industries KLM E&M. This represents a strategic gap and investment opportunity under MAIB 2030.
